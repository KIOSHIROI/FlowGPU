## Default coverage by platform

A profile's `allowed_kernel_kinds` is a *measured* policy: refusing a
kind is how the compiler says "no device evidence for this lowering on
this target yet", not a claim that the kind is unsupported.  The CANN
generator uses a wider Ascend profile (`tools/cann_bench/gen_from_dsl.py`
`ASCEND_KINDS`) for operators it has hardware evidence for.

| platform | allowed kinds | operators allowed | operators refused |
|---|---|---|---|
| cuda | 20 | 84/87 | 3 |
| cuda-tuned | 20 | 84/87 | 3 |
| ascend | 1 | 7/87 | 80 |
| ascend-wide | 20 | 84/87 | 3 |
| kunlunxin | 1 | 7/87 | 80 |

**cuda refuses:** `attention`, `composed`

**ascend refuses:** `attention`, `axis_gather`, `cache_store`, `composed`, `index_table`, `layout`, `linear_decode`, `matmul`, `pad_cat`, `patchify`, `primitive`, `recurrence`, `reduce_with_index`, `rope`, `rowwise`, `scan`, `scatter_by_index`, `segmented_matmul`, `store_at`, `topk`, `transpose`

**kunlunxin refuses:** `attention`, `axis_gather`, `cache_store`, `composed`, `index_table`, `layout`, `linear_decode`, `matmul`, `pad_cat`, `patchify`, `primitive`, `recurrence`, `reduce_with_index`, `rope`, `rowwise`, `scan`, `scatter_by_index`, `segmented_matmul`, `store_at`, `topk`, `transpose`
